# 🎵 Music Theory Hub

An interactive, responsive educational platform featuring **48 video lessons** and **38 interactive quizzes** across musical eras, elements, styles, and instruments.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://jsokar.github.io/music-theory/](https://jsokar.github.io/music-theory/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎬 48 Curated Video Lessons**: Spanning Historical Eras, Musical Elements (Beat, Meter, Duration, Notes & Rests, Musical Alphabet, Lines & Spaces, Pitch, Melody), Styles & Genres, and Instruments.
- **📝 38 Interactive Quizzes**: Integrated directly with real-time scoring, instant answer feedback, synth sound effects, audio clips, and score summary reports.
- **🧭 Persistent Left Navigation**: Easily switch between categories and filter by content type (Videos / Quizzes) or difficulty level (Levels 1–4).
- **🎛️ Dual-Mode Video Playback**:
  - Automatically streams embedded Google Drive previews on the web.
  - Plays crisp local MP4 videos when opened locally.
- **🔗 Seamless Interactivity**: Jump directly between any video lesson and its companion quiz with one click.
- **📱 Responsive Design**: Fully responsive layout tailored for desktops, Chromebooks, tablets, and Google Sites embedding.

---

## 📂 Categories Covered

1. **Musical Elements (47 items)**: Beat, Meter, Duration, Notes and Rests, Rhythm, Musical Alphabet, Lines and Spaces, Pitch, Melody, Harmony, Expressive Elements, and Form.
2. **Historical Eras (19 items)**: Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and Impressionist periods.
3. **Instruments & Stories (11 items)**: Peter and the Wolf, Keyboard Family, Cristofori's Pianoforte, The String Quartet, Tuba & Piccolo, Jambo Bwana, and Bananas.
4. **Styles & Genres (9 items)**: Blues, Jazz, Country, Pop, Rock, Reggae, Folk, and Musicals.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Simply double-click `Music-Theory.html` or `index.html` in any web browser. No server or build step required.
